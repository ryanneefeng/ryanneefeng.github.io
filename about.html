About me

I’m Ryan, a sophomore at Cornell. I grew up in Baton Rouge, Louisiana, where I lived my whole life before heading to university.

I attended Baton Rouge Magnet High School, then spent my first year of college at Fordham in New York City before transferring to Cornell.

A small BRMHS fun fact: Scott and Neal Wu also attended my high school. Read about their connection to BRMHS.

A lot of time in the pool

Swimming was a big part of my life growing up. I swam competitively for fourteen years before retiring from competition at seventeen. During that time, I became a three-time state champion and competed in LHSAA Division I varsity swimming for four years.

I still enjoy swimming, along with weightlifting. At Cornell, I’m involved in the Barbell Club; at Fordham, I also participated in Heavyweight Crew.

Outside the classroom

When I’m relaxing, I enjoy gaming, good food, and spending time with friends. Two games I play are Valorant and Marvel Rivals.

I’m also involved in Cornell’s Poker Club and Math Club.

Life on campus

Alongside my classes and clubs, I work as a Student Career Advisor in Cornell’s College of Arts & Sciences. I help other students with their resumes and think through opportunities that fit their interests and experience.

If you’d like to talk about a shared interest, a project, or an opportunity, feel free to get in touch.
