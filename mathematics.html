Mathematics & direction

I’m interested in both the structure of mathematics and the ways it can help us understand uncertainty and make decisions.

At Cornell, I’m on track to complete my mathematics major with concentrations in both Statistics and Applied Mathematics. My interests also extend to pure mathematics and the reasoning behind proofs.

What draws me in

Probability, statistics & stochastic processes
I’m interested in uncertainty, randomness, and how mathematical models describe them. My projects have given me opportunities to work with Monte Carlo simulation and statistical modeling.
Optimization & algorithms
I enjoy exploring how mathematical ideas become computational methods, and how those methods can support decisions with real constraints.
Pure mathematics & proofs
I’m drawn to understanding why a result is true and how the reasoning fits together. I want to keep developing that side of my mathematical education alongside my applied interests.

A professor who inspired me

One of the people who most inspired my interest in mathematics is Professor Anantharam Raghuram, who taught my discrete mathematics class at Fordham.

I loved how passionate he was about teaching, and how visual he made topics that many people would consider very theoretical. His approach helped me connect with the ideas behind proofs and see how engaging abstract mathematics could be.

Where it started

Growing up, I competed in a range of STEM and mathematics competitions. In middle school, I qualified for MATHCOUNTS Nationals. I later continued through the AMC competitions and qualified for the AIME.

Looking ahead

I’m interested in quantitative research or trading, and possibly a PhD and mathematical research. Both paths are open to me, and I’m not sure yet where I’ll end up. I’m still a sophomore, so I’m giving myself time to explore the kinds of problems and work I enjoy.

For now, I’m building my mathematical foundation, learning through programming projects, and staying curious about different directions.
